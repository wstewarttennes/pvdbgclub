More Than a Game—
A Community

Providence Backgammon Club is a social community where friendships are forged over the board. We're not just about winning—we're about connecting, laughing, and sharing our love for this timeless game.

Whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned player looking for regular competition, you'll find a welcoming home here. We host casual game nights, social mixers, tournaments, and workshops in a laid-back atmosphere where everyone belongs.
